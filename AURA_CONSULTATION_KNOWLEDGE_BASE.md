## 2. Style Consultation Framework

When guiding clients through their consultation, Aura AI follows this 5-dimensional framework:

### A. Facial Architecture Matching
- **Round Face Shapes:** Recommend elongated layers, curtain bangs that graze below the cheekbone, and crown volume to add vertical balance.
- **Oval Face Shapes:** Highly versatile; accommodates blunt bobs, layered lobs, soft waves, and sweeping fringe.
- **Square / Angular Shapes:** Soft, wispy perimeter layers and textured waves to soften jawline angles.
- **Heart / Inverted Triangle Shapes:** Collarbone-length lobs or chin-length layers with side-swept bangs to balance the forehead and jaw.

### B. Texture & Density Realities
- **Fine Hair:** Recommends internal point-cutting, blunt perimeter lines, and lightweight volumizing blowout techniques; avoids over-thinning.
- **Thick / Heavy Hair:** Recommends slide-cutting and soft interior layering to remove bulk without sacrificing length.
- **Wavy / Curly Hair:** Advises hydrating treatments, curl-by-curl cutting, sulfate-free conditioning, and diffusers.

### C. Color Undertone Harmony
- **Warm Skin Undertones:** Honey, golden beige, rich caramel, chocolate brown, warm auburn.
- **Cool Skin Undertones:** Ash brown, mocha, cool espresso, champagne blonde, burgundy.
- **Neutral Undertones:** Broad versatility spanning neutral beige, soft bronde, and balanced toffee.

### D. Maintenance Commitment Matrix
- **Low Maintenance:** Soft balayage (root-shadowed), long layers, air-dry textures. Refresh frequency: 12–16 weeks.
- **Moderate Upkeep:** Layered lobs, dimension babylights, regular hair spa rituals. Refresh frequency: 8–10 weeks.
- **High Commitment:** Vibrant global tones, precision blunt bobs, micro-bangs. Refresh frequency: 4–6 weeks.
